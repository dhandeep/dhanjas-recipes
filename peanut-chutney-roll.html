Peanut Chutney Roti Roll

◆ Night prep: make chutney ahead ◆ Morning: 10 min ◆ Serves 2 adults + toddler
~18–22g protein per adult (2 rolls) — peanuts + wheat
🌙 Night Before
  • Make the dry peanut chutney (step 1) — it keeps for weeks, so one batch covers many mornings
  • Optionally make the dough for the rotis — keeps overnight in the fridge
Ingredients
Rolls (per adult: 2)
  • Whole wheat roti2 per adult
  • Dry peanut chutney (pudi)2 tbsp per roti
  • Ghee or butter½ tsp per roti
  • Yogurt, to loosen the chutney (optional)1 tsp per roti
Filling options (per roll)
  • Paneer, thinly sliced or crumbled30–40g per roll
  • Cucumber, julienned2–3 tbsp per roll
  • Capsicum, thinly sliced2–3 tbsp per roll
  • Thick yogurt/hung curd, binder1 tbsp per roll
For dry peanut chutney
  • Roasted peanuts, skinned1 cup
  • Dry red chili2 (adults)
  • Cumin seeds½ tsp
  • Hing¼ tsp
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Lemon juice or aamchur1 tsp
Method
1 Make dry chutney (~10 min)
  • Pulse roasted peanuts (1 cup) in a dry blender — 5–6 short pulses until coarse crumble; do not over-blend into paste
  • Add cumin seeds (½ tsp), hing (¼ tsp), salt (½ tsp) and aamchur (1 tsp); pulse 2–3 times — keep it coarse and sandy, not smooth
  • Set aside (¼ cup) in a small jar for the toddler now, before the chili goes in
  • Add dry red chili (2) to the rest; pulse 2–3 more times to combine
  • Store both in airtight jars — 2–3 weeks at room temperature
2 Morning assembly
  • Heat rotis (2) on tawa — 30 sec per side; smear ghee (½ tsp) on each while warm
  • Spread dry chutney (2 tbsp) over each roti — if too dry, mix in yogurt (1 tsp) first
  • Add filling: paneer (30–40g), cucumber (2–3 tbsp), capsicum (2–3 tbsp), bound with thick yogurt (1 tbsp)
  • Roll tightly and serve immediately or wrap in foil
👶 Toddler version
  • Use her chili-free chutney from the small jar
  • Mix dry chutney (1 tsp) with yogurt (1 tbsp) to make a mild creamy spread
  • Spread on soft roti (1) cut into strips or small pieces — easier for her to manage than a roll
  • Add soft paneer pieces (a few soft pieces) or thinly sliced cucumber (a few thin slices) inside
Tip The chutney is the protein workhorse here — 2 tbsp of dry peanut chutney has ~7–8g protein. Double-layer it for more flavor and protein.
