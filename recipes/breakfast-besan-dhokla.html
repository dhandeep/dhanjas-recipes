A Sunday-batch steamed chickpea cake with a tangy mustard-seed tempering.

Instant Besan Dhokla

◆ Batch: 40 min (make Sunday) ◆ Morning: 5 min reheat ◆ Makes ~20 pieces
~16–18g protein per adult serving
🌙 Batch on Sunday
  • Make the full batch Sunday — keeps refrigerated 4–5 days
  • Reheat: microwave with a sprinkle of water, 60–90 sec, covered loosely
Ingredients
Batter
  • Besan (chickpea flour)2 cups
  • Full-fat yogurt½ cup
  • Water¾ cup
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 tsp
  • Green chili paste1 tsp (adults)
  • Lemon juice2 tbsp
Spices & leavening
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Turmeric½ tsp
  • Hing¼ tsp
  • Eno fruit salt, plain2 tsp total
Tempering
  • Oil2 tbsp
  • Mustard seeds1 tsp
  • Curry leaves10–12
  • Green chili, slit2 (adults)
  • Water3 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Lemon juice1 tbsp
Garnish
  • Fresh coriander, chopped2 tbsp
  • Fresh coconut, grated, optional2 tbsp
Method (Sunday batch)
1 Make batter
  • Whisk besan (2 cups) with yogurt (½ cup) and water (¾ cup) — no lumps
  • Add ginger (1 tsp), turmeric (½ tsp), hing (¼ tsp), lemon juice (2 tbsp), sugar (1 tsp), salt (1 tsp)
  • Split batter now — remove ¾ cup into a separate dish for toddler before adding chili
  • Add green chili paste (1 tsp) to adult batter only; rest both 5 min
👶 Toddler batch
  • Her ¾ cup is already set aside — mild, chili-free
  • Add Eno (½ tsp), pour into greased katori; steam 8–10 min (thinner layer)
2 Steam (Instant Pot)
  • Add 1½ cups water to IP inner pot; place trivet
  • Add Eno (1½ tsp) to adult batter — stir quickly until foamy; do not overmix
  • Pour immediately into greased tin (~1 inch layer); place on trivet
  • Steam 12–15 min with valve open — toothpick should come out clean
  • Cool 5 min before cutting
3 Tempering
  • Heat oil (2 tbsp); add mustard seeds (1 tsp) — splutter
  • Add curry leaves (10–12) and green chili (2, slit)
  • Add water (3 tbsp), sugar (1 tsp), lemon juice (1 tbsp); quick boil
  • Pour evenly over cut dhokla; let soak 5 min
  • Garnish with coriander (2 tbsp) and coconut (2 tbsp)
👶 Toddler reheat
  • Reheat her pieces separately — microwave (45 sec) with a splash of water
  • Serve in small pieces; pair with plain yogurt or warm milk
Key to fluffy dhokla Add Eno at the last second and get batter into the steamer within 60 seconds — the reaction is fast. Waiting = flat dhokla.
