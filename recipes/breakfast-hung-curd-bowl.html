Hung Curd Protein Bowl

◆ Night prep: 5 min (hang curd) ◆ Morning: 5 min assembly ◆ Serves 2 adults + toddler
~22–28g protein per adult bowl
🌙 Night Before — essential
  • Line colander with muslin cloth; pour in yogurt (500g); tie and hang over a bowl in the fridge
  • By morning: thick hung curd with ~2× protein density; save drained whey for roti dough
Ingredients
Base & toppings (per bowl)
  • Hung curd (from 250g yogurt)~¾ cup
  • Hemp seeds2 tbsp
  • Pumpkin seeds2 tbsp
  • Almonds, roughly chopped8–10
  • Walnuts, broken4–5 halves
Flavor — sweet version
  • Honey or jaggery powder1 tsp
  • Seasonal fruit½ cup
Flavor — savory version
  • Roasted cumin powder¼ tsp
  • Black salt (kala namak)a pinch
  • Cucumber, finely diced¼ cup
  • Coriander leaves1 tbsp
To serve alongside
  • Multigrain toast or makhanaa handful
Method
1 Morning assembly
  • Unwrap hung curd — should be thick like labneh
  • Divide into two adult bowls (~¾ cup each)
  • Sweet: drizzle honey (1 tsp), add fruit (½ cup), top with seeds and nuts
  • Savory: stir in kala namak and cumin (¼ tsp), top with cucumber (¼ cup), coriander, seeds and nuts
👶 Toddler bowl
  • Use regular full-fat yogurt (not hung) — easier texture
  • Add mashed banana or soft fruit (¼ cup)
  • Top with finely ground almonds (1 tsp) — no whole nuts (choking hazard)
  • Drizzle honey — fine for age 2+
  • Optional: mash in a small boiled potato for extra calories
Protein math Hung curd yields ~10–12g protein per 100g. Hemp seeds add 10g per 3 tbsp. Together with nuts you're at 22–28g with zero cooking.
