Hung Curd Protein Bowl

◆ Night prep: 5 min (hang curd) ◆ Morning: 5 min assembly ◆ Serves 2 adults + toddler
~22–28g protein per adult bowl
🌙 Night Before — essential
  • Hang the yogurt (step 1) — 5 min of work, but it needs the whole night in the fridge
Ingredients
Base & toppings
  • Full-fat yogurt, to hang (makes ~1½ cups hung curd)500g
  • Hemp seeds2 tbsp per bowl
  • Pumpkin seeds2 tbsp per bowl
  • Almonds, roughly chopped8–10 per bowl
  • Walnuts, broken4–5 halves per bowl
  • Multigrain toast or makhana, to servea handful
Flavor — sweet or savory
  • Jaggery powder1 tsp
  • Seasonal fruit½ cup
  • Roasted cumin powder¼ tsp
  • Black salt (kala namak)a pinch
  • Cucumber, finely diced¼ cup
  • Coriander leaves1 tbsp
Toddler bowl
  • Full-fat yogurt, not hung⅓ cup
  • Banana or soft fruit, mashed¼ cup
  • Boiled potato, optional1 small
Method
1 Hang the curd (night before)
  • Line a colander with muslin cloth and set it over a deep bowl
  • Pour in yogurt (500g); tie the cloth and hang it over the bowl in the fridge overnight
  • By morning it's thick hung curd with ~2× the protein density
  • Save the drained whey for roti dough
2 Morning assembly
  • Unwrap the hung curd (~1½ cups) — should be thick like labneh
  • Divide into two adult bowls (~¾ cup each)
  • Sweet: stir in jaggery powder (1 tsp), add fruit (½ cup), top with hemp seeds (2 tbsp), pumpkin seeds (2 tbsp), almonds (8–10) and walnuts (4–5 halves)
  • Savory: stir in kala namak (a pinch) and cumin (¼ tsp), top with cucumber (¼ cup), coriander (1 tbsp), hemp seeds (2 tbsp), pumpkin seeds (2 tbsp), almonds (8–10) and walnuts (4–5 halves)
  • Serve multigrain toast or makhana (a handful) alongside
👶 Toddler bowl
  • Use regular full-fat yogurt (⅓ cup) (not hung) — easier texture
  • Add mashed banana or soft fruit (¼ cup)
  • Top with finely ground almonds (1 tsp) — no whole nuts (choking hazard)
  • Sweeten with jaggery powder (a pinch)
  • Optional: mash in boiled potato (1 small) for extra calories
Protein math Hung curd yields ~10–12g protein per 100g. Hemp seeds add 10g per 3 tbsp. Together with nuts you're at 22–28g with zero cooking.
