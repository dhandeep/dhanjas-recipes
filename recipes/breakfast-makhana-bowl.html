Makhana Trail Mix Bowl

◆ Night prep: roast makhana ahead ◆ Morning: 3 min assembly ◆ Serves 2 adults + toddler
~16–20g protein per adult bowl (makhana + nuts + seeds)
🌙 Batch Roast (lasts 1–2 weeks)
  • Roast makhana in a dry pan on low heat 8–10 min until crisp; season lightly with ghee and salt
  • Cool completely before storing in an airtight jar — stays crisp for 2 weeks
Ingredients
Per adult bowl
  • Roasted makhana (fox nuts)1 cup
  • Almonds8–10
  • Walnuts, broken4–5 halves
  • Pumpkin seeds1 tbsp
  • Hemp seeds1 tbsp
  • Raisins or dried cranberries1 tbsp
To serve alongside
  • Full-fat yogurt or hung curd½ cup
  • Fresh fruit½ cup
  • Honey1 tsp
For roasting makhana
  • Makhana (fox nuts), raw3–4 cups
  • Ghee1 tsp
  • Salta pinch
  • Black pepper, optionala pinch
Method
1 Roast makhana (batch, not daily)
  • Heat a wide heavy pan on low — no oil yet
  • Add makhana (3–4 cups); dry roast, stirring constantly, 8–10 min until they feel light and crisp when pressed
  • Add ghee (1 tsp), salt (pinch), black pepper if using; toss well for 1 min
  • Cool completely on a plate; store in an airtight jar once fully cool
2 Morning assembly
  • Add roasted makhana (1 cup) to bowl
  • Add almonds (8–10), walnuts (4–5 halves), pumpkin seeds (1 tbsp), hemp seeds (1 tbsp), raisins (1 tbsp)
  • Serve yogurt (½ cup) and fruit (½ cup) alongside for dipping, with a drizzle of honey (1 tsp) if desired — keeps the makhana crisp
👶 Toddler bowl
  • Makhana is safe — soft enough once chewed, not a choking risk at age 2
  • Give whole makhana (¼ cup) — she'll enjoy the crunch
  • Serve with yogurt for dipping — makes it interactive and fun
  • Grind almonds finely (1 tsp) before adding; skip whole walnuts (too hard to chew)
  • Add soft fruit pieces (¼ cup) to round it out
Tip Serve the yogurt and makhana in separate bowls — pouring yogurt over makes makhana go soggy fast. Dip and eat as you go.
