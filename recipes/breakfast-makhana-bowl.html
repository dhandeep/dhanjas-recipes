Makhana Trail Mix Bowl

◆ Night prep: roast makhana ahead ◆ Morning: 3 min assembly ◆ Serves 2 adults + toddler
~16–20g protein per adult bowl (makhana + nuts + seeds)
🌙 Batch Roast (lasts 1–2 weeks)
  • Roast the makhana ahead (step 1) — one batch lasts 1–2 weeks
  • Cool completely before storing in an airtight jar — stays crisp for 2 weeks
Ingredients
Batch roast
  • Makhana (fox nuts), raw3–4 cups
  • Ghee1 tsp
  • Salta pinch
Per adult bowl
  • Roasted makhana1 cup per bowl
  • Almonds8–10 per bowl
  • Walnuts, broken4–5 halves per bowl
  • Pumpkin seeds1 tbsp per bowl
  • Hemp seeds1 tbsp per bowl
  • Raisins or dried cranberries1 tbsp per bowl
To season & serve alongside
  • Black pepper, optional (adults)a pinch
  • Full-fat yogurt or hung curd½ cup per bowl
  • Fresh fruit½ cup per bowl
  • Jaggery powder, optional1 tsp per bowl
Method
1 Roast makhana (batch, not daily)
  • Heat a wide heavy pan on low — no oil yet
  • Add makhana (3–4 cups); dry roast, stirring constantly, 8–10 min until they feel light and crisp when pressed
  • Add ghee (1 tsp) and salt (a pinch); toss well for 1 min — no pepper, so the jar suits the toddler too
  • Cool completely on a plate; store in an airtight jar once fully cool
👶 Toddler bowl
  • Makhana is safe — soft enough once chewed, not a choking risk at age 2
  • Give whole roasted makhana (¼ cup) — she'll enjoy the crunch
  • Serve with yogurt (a small bowl) for dipping — makes it interactive and fun
  • Grind almonds (1 tsp) finely before adding; skip whole walnuts (too hard to chew)
  • Add soft fruit pieces (¼ cup)
2 Morning assembly (adults)
  • Add roasted makhana (1 cup) to each bowl
  • Add almonds (8–10), walnuts (4–5 halves), pumpkin seeds (1 tbsp), hemp seeds (1 tbsp), raisins (1 tbsp)
  • Sprinkle black pepper (a pinch) over and toss, if using
  • Serve yogurt (½ cup) and fruit (½ cup) alongside for dipping, with jaggery powder (1 tsp) if desired — keeps the makhana crisp
Tip Serve the yogurt and makhana in separate bowls — pouring yogurt over makes makhana go soggy fast. Dip and eat as you go.
