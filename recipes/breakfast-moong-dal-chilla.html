Moong Dal Chilla

◆ Night prep: 5 min (soak) ◆ Morning: 25 min ◆ Makes 6–7 chillas
~18–22g protein per adult serving (3 chillas)
🌙 Night Before
  • Rinse moong dal and soak in water overnight (minimum 6 hrs)
  • Blend and refrigerate batter — takes 5 min; set toddler portion aside before adding chili
Ingredients
Batter
  • Yellow moong dal (split, skinned)1 cup
  • Water (for blending)⅓–½ cup
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 tsp
  • Green chili, finely chopped1 (adults only)
  • Cumin seeds½ tsp
  • Hing (asafoetida)¼ tsp
  • Turmeric¼ tsp
  • Salt½ tsp
Fillings (optional)
  • Spinach, finely chopped¼ cup
  • Capsicum, finely diced¼ cup
  • Paneer, crumbled½ cup
  • Coriander leaves, chopped2 tbsp
For cooking
  • Ghee or oil1 tsp per chilla
Method
1 Make batter (night before)
  • Drain soaked moong dal completely
  • Blend with water (⅓ cup) until smooth — should coat a spoon but pour easily
  • Add ginger (1 tsp), cumin seeds (½ tsp), hing (¼ tsp), turmeric (¼ tsp), salt (½ tsp)
  • Fold in spinach (¼ cup) and capsicum (¼ cup) by hand — do not blend
👶 Toddler — set aside now (night before)
  • Remove ⅓ cup of batter before adding green chili
  • Stir in a pinch of turmeric and hing (tiny pinch) — skip green chili only
  • Optionally stir in crumbled paneer (2 tbsp) for extra protein
  • Store separately, labelled, in fridge
2 Cook (morning)
  • Stir batter well — add water (1–2 tbsp) if too thick
  • Stir green chili (1, chopped) into adult batter
  • Heat tawa on medium; grease with ghee (½ tsp)
  • Pour (~¼ cup) and spread in a circle; cook 2–3 min until edges lift
  • Drizzle ghee (½ tsp) around edges, flip, cook 1–2 min more
  • Cook toddler batter on medium-low first — smaller chillas, serve flat
👶 Toddler cooking
  • Cook on medium-low; make 2 small chillas (2–3 tbsp batter each)
  • Press gently with spatula — no aggressive flipping
  • Serve with plain yogurt or mild coriander chutney
Tip If chillas tear, the tawa isn't hot enough — wait 30 seconds before spreading.
