A quick semolina upma, roasted golden and studded with peanuts.

Semolina

Rava Upma with Peanuts

◆ Night prep: none ◆ Morning: 20 min ◆ Serves 2 adults + toddler
~14–16g protein per adult serving (peanuts are the primary source)
Ingredients
Main
  • Rava (semolina / sooji)1 cup
  • Raw peanuts⅓ cup
  • Water2¾ cups
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 tsp
  • Green chili, slit1–2 (adults)
  • Ghee2 tbsp
Tempering
  • Mustard seeds1 tsp
  • Cumin seeds½ tsp
  • Hing¼ tsp
  • Curry leaves8–10
  • Cashews, optional10–12
Vegetables
  • Capsicum, finely diced½ cup
  • Tomato, chopped1 medium
  • Turmeric¼ tsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Lemon juice1 tsp
  • Fresh coriander2 tbsp
  • Boiled potato, finely diced (toddler only)1–2 tbsp
Method
1 Dry roast rava and peanuts
  • Heat a wide pan on medium; add rava (1 cup) — dry roast 3–4 min, stirring constantly until lightly golden and fragrant
  • Remove and set aside
  • In the same pan, dry roast peanuts (⅓ cup) on medium 3–4 min until skins blister; set aside
  • Let the peanuts cool, then rub off any loose skins
2 Tempering
  • Heat ghee (2 tbsp) in the pan on medium
  • Add mustard seeds (1 tsp) — splutter; add cumin seeds (½ tsp)
  • Add hing (¼ tsp), curry leaves (8–10), cashews (10–12) if using — stir 30 sec
  • Add ginger (1 tsp) — cook 20 sec
  • Add capsicum (½ cup) and tomato (1 medium); cook 2 min
  • Add turmeric (¼ tsp) and salt (to taste); stir
👶 Toddler — pull portion here (before chili)
  • Scoop out the cooked vegetables (3–4 tbsp) into a separate small pan now — before adding green chili to the main pan
  • Add water (¼ cup) and roasted rava (3 tbsp) to her pan; cook on low, stirring until soft and water absorbed
  • Stir in crushed peanuts (1 tsp) or ghee (a drop)
  • Optional: add finely diced boiled potato (1–2 tbsp) for extra bulk
3 Add chili and cook upma
  • Add green chili (1–2) to the adult pan; cook 20 sec
  • Bring water (2½ cups) to a boil in a separate pot; pour into the tempering pan carefully
  • Reduce heat to medium-low; slowly add remaining roasted rava (¾ cup + 1 tbsp) while stirring continuously to avoid lumps
  • Stir in remaining roasted peanuts (5 tbsp)
  • Cover and cook 3–4 min on low until rava absorbs all water
  • Finish with lemon juice (1 tsp) and coriander (2 tbsp)
Tip Roasting rava before cooking is non-negotiable — it prevents lumpy upma. Add water slowly and stir constantly as it goes in.
