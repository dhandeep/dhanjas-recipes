Crunchy Dumpling Lettuce Wraps

Korean-inspired lettuce wraps with a Jain TVP filling, homemade gochujang, and crunchy dumpling strips.

TVP · Fusion

Crunchy Dumpling Lettuce Wraps

◆ 4 servings ◆ ~50 min ◆ ≈14 lettuce cups
~20g protein per adult serving — from TVP, plus peanuts in the chili crisp
Ingredients
TVP base
  • Dry TVP (granules/chunks)150g
  • Hot water2 cups
  • Reduced-sodium soy sauce1½ tbsp
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 tsp
  • Hing¼ tsp
  • Sugar½ tsp
Jain gochujang (makes ⅔ cup)
  • Gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)3 tbsp
  • White miso paste2 tbsp
  • Light brown sugar or jaggery2 tbsp
  • Rice vinegar1 tbsp
  • Warm water3 tbsp
  • Salt¼ tsp
Jain chili crisp (makes ½ cup)
  • Neutral oil (peanut or canola)⅓ cup
  • Fresh ginger, finely julienned1 tbsp
  • Gochugaru or Kashmiri chili flakes2 tbsp
  • Sesame seeds1 tbsp
  • Roasted peanuts, crushed2 tbsp
  • Hing¼ tsp
  • Sugar½ tsp
  • Salt½ tsp
Sauce
  • Reduced-sodium soy sauce5 tbsp
  • Water6 tbsp
  • Toasted sesame oil2 tbsp
  • Light brown sugar2 tbsp
  • Cornstarch1 tbsp
  • Jain gochujang (from above)2 tbsp
Filling
  • Neutral oil2 tbsp
  • Hing¼ tsp
  • Celery, sliced thin diagonal2 stalks
  • Fresh ginger, grated2 tbsp
  • Green cabbage, shredded~4 cups
  • Red bell pepper, julienned1 medium
Crunchy dumpling strips
  • Eggless dumpling wrappers14
  • Neutral oil, for frying1 tbsp
  • White pepper¼ tsp
  • Dried mushroom powder, optional½ tsp
  • Sugar¼ tsp
  • Salt¼ tsp
Assembly
  • Butter lettuce2 heads
  • Toasted sesame seeds2 tbsp
  • Celery leaves or coriander¼ cup
  • Jain chili crisp (from above)to taste
Method
1 Soak the TVP
  • Stir together hot water (2 cups), soy sauce (1½ tbsp), grated ginger (1 tsp), hing (¼ tsp) and sugar (½ tsp) in a large bowl
  • Add the dry TVP (150g) and stir well
  • Cover and set aside for 15–20 minutes. It returns in Step 8 with all its liquid.
2 Make the Jain gochujang
  • In a small bowl, whisk gochugaru (3 tbsp — or 2 tbsp Kashmiri chili powder + 1 tbsp regular chili powder), white miso (2 tbsp), brown sugar (2 tbsp), rice vinegar (1 tbsp) and salt (¼ tsp)
  • Add warm water (3 tbsp) a spoonful at a time, whisking, until it forms a thick glossy paste that falls slowly off the spoon
  • Let it rest 10 minutes so the chili flakes hydrate and the raw edge softens
  • Makes about ⅔ cup — you need 2 tbsp today, the rest keeps refrigerated for a month
3 Make the Jain chili crisp
  • Put gochugaru (2 tbsp), sesame seeds (1 tbsp), crushed peanuts (2 tbsp), hing (¼ tsp), sugar (½ tsp) and salt (½ tsp) into a heatproof bowl
  • Heat neutral oil (⅓ cup) with the julienned ginger (1 tbsp) in a small pan over medium heat, 4–5 minutes, until the ginger is pale gold and the bubbling slows
  • Take the pan off the heat, wait 30 seconds, then pour the oil and ginger over the bowl — it will sizzle hard for a few seconds
  • Stir and cool completely. Set aside — it goes on at serving, Step 10.
4 Mix the sauce, reserve the toddler's share
  • Whisk soy sauce (5 tbsp), water (6 tbsp), toasted sesame oil (2 tbsp), brown sugar (2 tbsp) and cornstarch (1 tbsp) until completely smooth, no lumps
  • Scoop 3 tbsp of this plain sauce into a small bowl and set it aside — that's the toddler's, and it returns in her portion right after Step 8, before the gochujang goes in
  • Now stir the Jain gochujang (2 tbsp) into the sauce left in the big bowl. Set aside.
5 Prep everything else
  • Slice celery (2 stalks) thin on a sharp diagonal — you want long shards, not chunks
  • Shred the cabbage (~4 cups), julienne the red bell pepper (1 medium), grate the ginger (2 tbsp)
  • Julienne a small carrot and keep it in a separate bowl — toddler only. Microwave it with water (1 tbsp), covered, 2 minutes until just tender; drain and set aside — it returns in her portion right after Step 8.
  • Stack the dumpling wrappers (14) and cut into strips about ½ cm wide
  • Separate, wash and thoroughly dry the butter lettuce (2 heads) into cups — wet leaves make the filling slide out
6 Crisp the dumpling strips
  • Air fryer (recommended): toss the strips with oil (1 tbsp) until barely slicked. Spread in a single layer in the Ninja Crispi and run 330°F for 4–6 minutes, shaking at the 3-minute mark. Pull them while still pale gold — they darken and crisp further as they cool.
  • Deep-fry alternative: heat oil (2 cups) in a heavy saucepan over high heat; fry the strips in batches, stirring gently, until golden. Lift onto a lined plate.
  • Set the finished strips aside — they get seasoned next, then go on at serving, Step 10
👶 Toddler — pull her strips now, before seasoning
  • Take a small handful of the crisped strips out and put them on her plate
  • Salt them very lightly and leave them alone otherwise
  • Do this before the next step — white pepper is mild but there's no reason for her to have it
7 Season the adult strips
  • Toss the remaining strips with white pepper (¼ tsp), mushroom powder (½ tsp), sugar (¼ tsp) and salt (¼ tsp) while still warm
8 Cook the filling
  • Heat neutral oil (2 tbsp) in a large sauté pan over medium-high heat
  • Add hing (¼ tsp) and swirl for 10 seconds until it smells savory and nutty — don't let it brown
  • Add the celery (2 stalks) and grated ginger (2 tbsp). Cook 2 minutes — the celery should stay squeaky, not go soft.
  • Add the shredded cabbage (~4 cups) and red bell pepper (1 medium). Cook 5–7 minutes until the cabbage wilts and the pepper just softens.
  • Add the soaked TVP (150g) from Step 1 along with all its remaining liquid. Stir through and cook 2 minutes. Stop here — nothing spicy has gone in yet.
👶 Toddler — pull her filling now, before the gochujang sauce
  • Scoop about 1 cup of the filling and TVP out of the pan into a small saucepan
  • Add the softened carrot from Step 5
  • Pour in the plain reserved sauce (3 tbsp) from Step 4
  • Simmer 2 minutes, stirring, until it thickens and coats everything
  • Finish off the heat with ghee (½ tsp)
  • Serve with her plain salted strips from earlier, and either torn butter lettuce or a small bowl of rice
  • Hing and ginger are already in this and both are fine for her — the only things she's missing are the gochujang and the chili crisp; everything else is identical to the adult version, carrot included
9 Finish the adult filling
  • Pour the gochujang sauce from Step 4 over what's left in the big pan
  • Cook 5–7 minutes, stirring, until the sauce is glossy and thick enough to sit in a lettuce leaf without running out the bottom
  • Taste and adjust salt — it should be sweet-salty first, heat second
10 Assemble
  • Spoon the filling into the butter lettuce cups from Step 5
  • Top each with the seasoned dumpling strips from Step 7, toasted sesame seeds (2 tbsp) and chopped celery leaves (¼ cup)
  • Finish with Jain chili crisp (to taste) from Step 3
  • Add the strips right at the table — they go soft within a couple of minutes of touching the sauce
What changed from the original Onion → celery, sliced thin, plus hing bloomed in the oil. Garlic → hing. Green onion → celery leaves or coriander. Carrot → red bell pepper for adults (real carrot stays only in the toddler's portion). Bouillon cube → soy sauce + ginger + hing in the soaking water — cubes almost always carry onion or garlic powder. Gochujang and chili crisp are made from scratch here since most jarred brands, including Lao Gan Ma, list garlic. "Chicken spice" → white pepper, sugar, salt, mushroom powder. Wrappers: buy Chinese-style (flour, water, salt) — some gyoza wrappers contain egg, check the packet.
Appliance notes Ninja Crispi for the strips: it runs hotter than most air fryers, 330°F is its floor and it's plenty here — start checking at 3 minutes, these go from pale to burnt in about 45 seconds. Wide sauté pan, not a wok, for the filling — you want the cabbage to wilt and lose water fast, a crowded pan steams it into mush. Small pan for the chili crisp — deep enough that ⅓ cup of oil is at least 2cm deep, or the ginger scorches before it crisps. Instant Pot isn't useful here — nothing benefits from pressure, and TVP just needs a soak.
Recipe notes Celery is doing real work — slice it thin on a steep diagonal; cut into rounds it reads as "celery," cut into shards it reads as the sweet allium note the original wanted. Salt check: there's soy sauce in both the TVP soak and the sauce, so taste before adding extra salt at Step 9. The gochujang improves overnight — if you make it a day ahead, the miso rounds out and the chili loses its raw bite. No dumpling wrappers? Cut flour tortillas or spring roll pastry into strips instead — same treatment, slightly less shattery. Leftovers: filling keeps 3 days refrigerated and reheats well; store the strips and chili crisp separately at room temperature, never in the fridge. Heat level is moderate as written — halve the gochujang for a milder version, or serve it on the side and let people stir their own in.
