Peanut Chutney Roti Roll

◆ Night prep: make chutney ahead ◆ Morning: 10 min ◆ Serves 2 adults + toddler
~18–22g protein per adult (2 rolls) — peanuts + wheat
🌙 Night Before
  • Make a batch of dry peanut chutney (shengdana chutney pudi) — keeps for weeks
  • Mix chutney with yogurt or ghee to make a spreadable paste; refrigerate
  • Optionally make roti dough — keeps overnight in the fridge
Ingredients
Per adult (2 rolls)
  • Whole wheat roti2
  • Dry peanut chutney (pudi)2 tbsp/roti
  • Ghee or butter½ tsp/roti
Filling options
  • Paneer, thinly sliced or crumbled30–40g/roll
  • Cucumber, julienned2–3 tbsp
  • Capsicum, thinly sliced2–3 tbsp
  • Thick yogurt/hung curd, binder1 tbsp
For dry peanut chutney
  • Roasted peanuts, skinned1 cup
  • Dry red chili2 (adults)
  • Cumin seeds½ tsp
  • Hing¼ tsp
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Lemon juice or aamchur1 tsp
Method
1 Make dry chutney (~10 min)
  • Pulse roasted peanuts (1 cup) in a dry blender — 5–6 short pulses until coarse crumble; do not over-blend into paste
  • Add dry red chili (2), cumin seeds (½ tsp), hing (¼ tsp), salt (½ tsp), aamchur (1 tsp)
  • Pulse 2–3 more times to combine — keep it coarse and sandy, not smooth
  • Stores in an airtight jar for 2–3 weeks at room temperature
2 Morning assembly
  • Heat roti on tawa — 30 sec per side; smear ghee (½ tsp) while warm
  • Spread dry chutney (2 tbsp) over roti — if too dry, mix a spoonful of yogurt into the chutney first
  • Add filling: paneer (30–40g), cucumber, capsicum
  • Roll tightly and serve immediately or wrap in foil
👶 Toddler version
  • Use chutney made without dry red chili — pulse peanuts with just cumin, hing, salt, aamchur
  • Mix (1 tsp) chutney with yogurt (1 tbsp) to make a mild creamy spread
  • Soft roti cut into strips or small pieces — easier for her to manage than a roll
  • Add soft paneer pieces or thinly sliced cucumber inside
Tip The chutney is the protein workhorse here — 2 tbsp of dry peanut chutney has ~7–8g protein. Double-layer it for more flavor and protein.
