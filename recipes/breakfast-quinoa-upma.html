Quinoa Upma

◆ Night prep: rinse and soak quinoa ◆ Morning: 20 min ◆ Serves 2 adults + toddler
~20–24g protein per adult serving — quinoa is a complete protein
🌙 Night Before
  • Rinse quinoa thoroughly in cold water until it runs clear (removes saponin bitterness)
  • Soak 15–20 min, drain, refrigerate — cuts morning cook time
Ingredients
Main
  • Quinoa, rinsed and soaked1 cup
  • Water1¾ cups
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 tsp
  • Green chili, slit1–2 (adults)
  • Ghee2 tbsp
  • Lemon juice1 tsp
Tempering
  • Mustard seeds1 tsp
  • Cumin seeds½ tsp
  • Hing¼ tsp
  • Curry leaves8–10
  • Cashews, optional10
Vegetables & finish
  • Capsicum, finely diced½ cup
  • Tomato, chopped1 medium
  • Green peas, frozen¼ cup
  • Turmeric¼ tsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Fresh coriander2 tbsp
Method
1 Tempering
  • Heat ghee (2 tbsp) in a heavy pan on medium
  • Add mustard seeds (1 tsp) — splutter; add cumin seeds (½ tsp)
  • Add hing (¼ tsp), curry leaves (8–10), cashews (10) — stir 30 sec
  • Add ginger (1 tsp) — cook 20 sec
  • Add capsicum (½ cup) and tomato (1 medium); cook 2 min
  • Add peas (¼ cup), turmeric (¼ tsp), salt; stir
👶 Toddler — pull portion here
  • Scoop out vegetables (3–4 tbsp) before adding chili to the main pan
  • Add water (⅓ cup) and rinsed quinoa (3 tbsp) to a small pan with her vegetables
  • Cover and cook on low 12 min until quinoa is soft and water absorbed
  • Mash lightly if needed; add a drop of ghee
2 Add chili and cook quinoa
  • Add green chili (1–2) to the adult pan; cook 20 sec
  • Add remaining quinoa to the tempering pan; stir to coat 1 min
  • Add water (1¾ cups) and bring to a boil
  • Reduce to low; cover and cook 12–14 min until water absorbed and quinoa tails appear
  • Rest covered 5 min; fluff with fork
  • Finish with lemon juice (1 tsp) and coriander (2 tbsp)
Tip The white "tail" curling out of each quinoa grain means it's cooked. Don't skip the 5-minute covered rest — it makes the texture fluffy rather than sticky.
